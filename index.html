FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What should beginners expect on their first visit?

Welcome! You'll get a full facility tour, a quick movement assessment, and a chance to train with a coach — all at no cost. We'll make sure you feel comfortable and confident before your first real session.

Do you offer personal training?

Absolutely. All our coaches are certified and specialize in different areas — strength, conditioning, mobility, and body recomposition. Pro and Elite memberships include personal training sessions.

What membership options are available?

We offer three tiers — Starter, Pro, and Elite — each designed for a different level of commitment. All memberships are month-to-month with no long-term contracts. See the Membership section above for full details.

Can I try the gym before joining?

Yes — we offer a free trial session for new members. Click "Claim Your Free Session" below and we'll set everything up for you. No credit card required.

What should I bring to my first workout?

Just comfortable athletic wear, clean indoor training shoes, a water bottle, and a towel. We provide everything else — including locks for the lockers.
